Why the board shape matters in PLAY PLINKO ONLINE

The board in PLAY PLINKO ONLINE is built as a triangle of staggered pegs. Each row gives the ball another chance to move left or right, and that repeating split creates the final outcome. More rows mean more possible end positions, while fewer rows create a narrower and faster game flow.

This structure is why the game feels random but still follows a pattern over many rounds. Most balls settle near the center, while the outer pockets are harder to reach and usually pay more. In simple terms, the board shape controls how often different results can appear.
